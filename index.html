Why Edison Robotics Centre

Built Like a Technology Company. Run Like an Academy.

Every class is designed around real tools, real projects and real competitions — so students don't just learn technology, they build with it.

Hands-on Learning

Every module is project-based — students build, test and iterate real robots, code and circuits rather than memorising theory.

Competition Coaching

From national challenges to international titles, our students are coached to compete — and win — on regional and world stages.

Certified Trainers

Every class is led by trained, experienced instructors who mentor students one project at a time, not just lecture from a slide.

AI & Robotics

From Arduino and Micro:Bit robotics to practical artificial intelligence — students work with the same technology shaping tomorrow.

Beginner to Advanced Coding

A structured pathway from Scratch and block-based coding through to Arduino, AI and real-world programming projects.

Small Class Sizes

Focused, low student-to-trainer ratios mean every child gets hands-on guidance — not just a seat in a crowded room.

Our Story

Nurturing Malaysia's Next Generation of Innovators

Edison Robotics Centre is a premier provider of STEM education, specialising in coding, robotics and innovative learning solutions for primary and secondary school students in Malaysia. With a mission to nurture young minds and equip them with essential 21st-century skills, we're building a generation of thinkers, problem-solvers and creators.
