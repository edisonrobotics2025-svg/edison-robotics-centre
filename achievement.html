National Recognition

Showcasing to Malaysia's Minister of Digital, 2026

A proud milestone for Edison Robotics Centre — our students showcased their robotics and coding projects directly to Malaysia's Minister of Digital YB Tuan Gobind Singh Deo, putting their hands-on learning on a national stage.

School Outreach

STEM Workshop — SJKT Gelang Patah, Johor Bahru

We ran another hands-on STEM and robotics workshop at SJKT Gelang Patah in Johor Bahru, giving students the chance to build, code and problem-solve with real hardware.

03

Competition Results

National Robotic League — Paradigm Mall, Johor Bahru

Our young roboticists took home 2nd and 3rd place at the National Robotic League held at Paradigm Mall, Johor Bahru — going head-to-head with teams from across the country on the big stage.

04

School Outreach

STEM Workshop — SJKT Lobak, Negeri Sembilan

Our trainers travelled to SJKT Lobak in Negeri Sembilan to run a hands-on STEM and robotics workshop, giving students outside our home state their first taste of building and coding real projects.

05

School Outreach

Micro:Bit Workshop — SJKT Bukit Serampang

We brought hands-on Micro:Bit coding to SJKT Bukit Serampang in Johor Bahru, Johor — running the workshop section by section to reach around 100 students with real, hands-on time on the boards.
